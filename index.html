How these numbers work
A snipe is a BIN listed at a third of the market price or less. Missing zero means the gap is about 10x, 100x or 1000x, the usual sign of a typo. Market is the lowest of: the middle of the three cheapest listings from other sellers, sales recorded in this browser, and the 3-day price (the median sale over the last 3 days for the same rarity, stars or pet level, from Coflnet). A snipe has to be a third of that or less. Solid: recent sales or three close listings back the market price. Likely: two other sellers list within 30% of each other. Check: one listing or listings that disagree (hidden by default). Always look at the item in game before buying. Sold/day is the item's average sales per day, from Coflnet. Items that sell less often than your minimum are hidden, since a cheap item nobody buys is hard to resell. Snipes listed more than 30 minutes ago are hidden: a real typo gets bought within minutes, so an older one usually isn't actually cheap. The page checks Hypixel every second once a refresh is due and shows new snipes from the newest page first. Hypixel only publishes new data about once a minute, and new BINs can't be bought for their first 20 seconds.
