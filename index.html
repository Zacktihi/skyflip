A snipe is a BIN listed at a third of the market price or less. Missing zero means the gap is about 10x, 100x or 1000x, the usual sign of a typo. Market is the middle of the three cheapest listings from other sellers, capped by recent sales. Solid: recent sales or three close listings back the market price. Likely: two other sellers list within 30% of each other. Check: one listing or listings that disagree (hidden by default). Always look at the item in game before buying. Snipes go fast and Hypixel's data is up to a minute old, so some will already be sold. New BINs can't be bought for their first 20 seconds.
