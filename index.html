How these numbers work
Investments are liquid items whose price has dropped well below a stable 30-day level: the kind of dip that tends to come back once whatever caused it passes, such as an event flooding the market or a mayor perk ending. Each scan checks the 250 most-listed plain auction house items and the 150 most-traded bazaar items worth over 100k (Stock of Stonks is always included), using their daily price history for the last month from Coflnet. The first visit takes about 4 minutes to load them all; after that they are cached for 6 hours. An item is shown when today's price is 12–60% below its normal price and its recent sales have dropped too, its price was steady before that, it wasn't already sliding, and it trades at least 10 a day. "Recent" means the current mayor's term so far (at least 2 days), so a drop caused by the mayor is compared with prices from before they took office. Those get a mayor dip tag; they often recover when the term ends. Events. The lines above the table show SkyBlock events from the last 7 days, happening now, and coming in the next 7 days, worked out from the SkyBlock calendar and the current mayor's perks: Year of the Seal and Year of the Pig (each comes around every 12 SkyBlock years and lasts the whole year; the Pig falls 6 years after the Seal), Spooky Festival, Season of Jerry, New Year, Traveling Zoo, Mining Fiesta, Fishing Festival and Diana's Mythological Ritual. Items an event hands out are always checked. If one is cheap because a running event is adding supply, it gets a green tag with the event's name, and its price is compared with before the event started; these usually recover after the event. If an event that adds more of it starts soon, it gets an orange tag and is never rated Strong, since the price can drop further first. Item cards. Hover any item name (in every tab) for a card with its live price, sales over the last 3 days, its last 30 days of prices, ChudHouse's own recorded history, the investment verdict and any events affecting it. Click to pin the card and get a link to the full chart on Coflnet. Own price history. ChudHouse records the price of every auction house and bazaar item every 30 minutes on GitHub's servers, whether or not the site is open: hourly prices for the last 14 days and a daily summary kept for good, in the data branch. Once an item has 20 days of its own history it is judged on that instead of Coflnet's (auction items still use Coflnet for how often they sell). Hover the 30-day line to see which source a row uses. If it recovers is the normal price: the typical daily average for bazaar items, and the typical lowest daily sale for auction items (a cautious BIN price). Profit is that minus tax (bazaar 1.25%; auction listing fee and claim tax) and what you pay now. On the bazaar a buy order is cheaper than the instant-buy price shown, just slower. Strong means a very steady price before the dip, no downward drift and at least 50 trades a day. Nothing here is guaranteed. A dip can also be the start of a lasting drop, for example after a game update makes an item easier to get, so check recent SkyBlock news before investing a lot.

How these numbers work
Quick buy copies /viewauction for that snipe in one click. In Minecraft press T, Ctrl+V and Enter, and the auction opens to buy. The floating button bottom-right always points at the newest snipe you haven't copied yet, and Q does the same from the keyboard. Copied snipes are greyed out. Nothing is bought automatically: Hypixel bans auto-buyers. Only snipes that make more than 250k profit after tax are shown. With both buttons on, BIN snipes are listed first and ending auctions in their own table underneath. Two kinds of snipe. BIN: a Buy It Now listing at a third of the market price or less. Ending auctions (tagged auction): a bid auction ending within 5 minutes whose next bid (about 15% above the current one) is two-thirds of the market price or less. Other players can still outbid you, and Hypixel's data is up to a minute old, so check the current bid in game. The scan reads auction pages from both ends inward and shows snipes from the first pages as soon as they arrive. A page that takes more than 5 seconds is skipped for that update so the rest isn't held up. A BIN snipe is a listing at a third of the market price or less. Missing zero means the gap is about 10x, 100x or 1000x, the usual sign of a typo. Market is the lowest of: the middle of the three cheapest listings from other sellers, sales recorded in this browser, and the 3-day price (the median sale over the last 3 days for the same rarity, stars or pet level, from Coflnet). A snipe has to be a third of that or less. Solid: recent sales or three close listings back the market price. Likely: two other sellers list within 30% of each other. Check: one listing or listings that disagree (hidden by default). Always look at the item in game before buying. Sold/day is the item's average sales per day, from Coflnet. Items that sell less often than your minimum are hidden, since a cheap item nobody buys is hard to resell. Snipes listed more than 30 minutes ago are hidden: a real typo gets bought within minutes, so an older one usually isn't actually cheap. The page checks Hypixel every second once a refresh is due and shows new snipes from the newest page first. Hypixel only publishes new data about once a minute, and new BINs can't be bought for their first 20 seconds.

How these numbers work
Shards only (on by default) limits the list to shards. A shard is shown when, after tax, the gap is between your minimum and maximum margin and trades at least your minimum amount per day, so the orders actually fill. Shard order books are thin and jumpy, which is why the 100% max margin matters most here. Bazaar flipping: place a buy order just above the highest one, and once it fills, a sell offer just below the lowest one. You earn the gap minus the bazaar tax, which is only charged when you sell. Profit / item = sell offer price × (1 − tax) − buy order price. Margin is that profit as a share of what you pay. Traded / day is the slower of the two sides over the last 7 days: how many items people instantly sold into buy orders, and how many they instantly bought from sell offers. Both your orders need that flow to fill. Profit / day (10%) assumes you fill a tenth of that daily flow. It's a way to rank items by realistic size, not a promise: other flippers compete for the same orders (see Orders). Very wide gaps on thinly traded items are often manipulated, so Max margin % hides anything above 100% by default; still check the item's price history in game before committing a lot of coins. Data comes straight from Hypixel and refreshes about every 20 seconds.

How these numbers work
All 181 talismans, rings and artifacts that are made at a crafting table, with their recipes and unlock requirements from the NotEnoughUpdates item repo. 98 of them need no collection or slayer level, so anyone can craft them. Crafting the parts. Every ingredient that has its own recipe is also priced as a craft, all the way down (for example Enchanted Ink Sac from 80 Ink Sacs, or a lower talisman from its own ingredients), and the cheaper of buying or crafting is used. Parts marked ⚒ are cheaper to craft; hover them to see what goes in and what buying would cost. Crafting a part can need its own collection level: those are listed under the requirement as "parts:". Choose "only recipes without requirement" to keep the whole chain requirement-free, or "buy every part" to price only the final craft. Craft cost adds up every ingredient at live prices: bazaar items at the instant-buy price (or your buy-order price if you switch above), and auction items, like the lower talisman in an upgrade, at their cheapest BIN. Profit is the talisman's cheapest BIN minus the listing fee and claim tax, minus the craft cost. A craft is shown when it makes a profit and every ingredient has a price. Ingredients that can't be bought (soulbound drops, items nobody is selling) make the recipe unpriceable; switch on "Unprofitable too" to see those. Check Sold/day and Listed before crafting a stack: a big profit on a talisman that sells once a day is hard to cash in.
