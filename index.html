How these numbers work
Only snipes that make more than 250k profit after tax are shown. With both buttons on, BIN snipes are listed first and ending auctions in their own table underneath. Two kinds of snipe. BIN: a Buy It Now listing at a third of the market price or less. Ending auctions (tagged auction): a bid auction ending within 5 minutes whose next bid (about 15% above the current one) is two-thirds of the market price or less. Other players can still outbid you, and Hypixel's data is up to a minute old, so check the current bid in game. The scan reads auction pages from both ends inward and shows snipes from the first pages as soon as they arrive. A page that takes more than 5 seconds is skipped for that update so the rest isn't held up. A BIN snipe is a listing at a third of the market price or less. Missing zero means the gap is about 10x, 100x or 1000x, the usual sign of a typo. Market is the lowest of: the middle of the three cheapest listings from other sellers, sales recorded in this browser, and the 3-day price (the median sale over the last 3 days for the same rarity, stars or pet level, from Coflnet). A snipe has to be a third of that or less. Solid: recent sales or three close listings back the market price. Likely: two other sellers list within 30% of each other. Check: one listing or listings that disagree (hidden by default). Always look at the item in game before buying. Sold/day is the item's average sales per day, from Coflnet. Items that sell less often than your minimum are hidden, since a cheap item nobody buys is hard to resell. Snipes listed more than 30 minutes ago are hidden: a real typo gets bought within minutes, so an older one usually isn't actually cheap. The page checks Hypixel every second once a refresh is due and shows new snipes from the newest page first. Hypixel only publishes new data about once a minute, and new BINs can't be bought for their first 20 seconds.

How these numbers work
Bazaar flipping: place a buy order just above the highest one, and once it fills, a sell offer just below the lowest one. You earn the gap minus the bazaar tax, which is only charged when you sell. Profit / item = sell offer price × (1 − tax) − buy order price. Margin is that profit as a share of what you pay. Traded / day is the slower of the two sides over the last 7 days: how many items people instantly sold into buy orders, and how many they instantly bought from sell offers. Both your orders need that flow to fill. Profit / day (10%) assumes you fill a tenth of that daily flow. It's a way to rank items by realistic size, not a promise: other flippers compete for the same orders (see Orders). Very wide gaps on thinly traded items are often manipulated, so Max margin % hides anything above 100% by default; still check the item's price history in game before committing a lot of coins. Data comes straight from Hypixel and refreshes about every 20 seconds.
